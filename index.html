Frequently asked questions about Tynkr browser tools

Why did kitchen and tabletop tools move to other subdomains?

Kitchen calculators moved to cooking.builtbyjoshstudio.com/tools/ alongside the recipes and videos, and tabletop utilities moved to writing.builtbyjoshstudio.com/tabletop/. Money and business calculators live here on tools.builtbyjoshstudio.com, with permanent redirects preserving old links.

Do any of these browser tools upload what I type or require an account?

No. All calculators and generators run client-side in your browser with no signup, no ads, and no server-side storage of your numbers or inputs.

How do the money calculators save inputs on my device?

Eleven money and business calculators store your inputs in this browser's localStorage under tool-specific keys and let you export parameters via Copy link. The Invoice Generator keeps letterhead images and line items strictly in memory with no localStorage and no link sharing.

Are downloadable spreadsheets or desktop apps available if I outgrow the web tools?

Yes. Built by Josh Studio publishes Tynkr Notion templates, Excel + Google Sheets workbooks, and Guided Series print planners at builtbyjoshstudio.com/products/, alongside offline Windows desktop software at tynkr.builtbyjoshstudio.com.
