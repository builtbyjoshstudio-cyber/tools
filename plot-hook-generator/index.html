Every session starts with one sentence

Campaigns are built out of single sentences that got out of hand — someone needs a thing moved, something is wrong with the water, the wrong person turned up alive. This generator produces those sentences, plus the complication that turns an errand into a story.

The twist does the heavy lifting

A hook without a twist is a fetch quest. The twist line is the part your players will actually remember — it's generated separately from the hook, which means the combinations land in places neither line would alone. Roll until two halves spark against each other.

Prompts, not prescriptions

Nothing here specifies a system, a level, or a stat block. A hook that reads as a back-alley job in one campaign is a royal conspiracy in another — the generator hands you the spark, and your table decides the size of the fire.

Are these hooks from published adventures?

No — every hook and twist is original invented content written for this generator. If one resembles a published adventure, that's coincidence; the combinations are assembled randomly in your browser.

How should I use the twist line?

However your table likes. Reveal it mid-session, hold it for the finale, or discard it and let the hook play straight — the twist is a suggestion for where the story bends, not a requirement.

Is this plot hook generator free?

Yes — free, no ads, no signup, and it runs entirely in your browser. Kept hooks are stored only on your own device.
