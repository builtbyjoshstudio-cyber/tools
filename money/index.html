Frequently asked questions about the Money & Business suite

Which tax year and brackets do Quarterly Tax Estimator and Take-Home Pay Calculator use?

Both calculators use 2026 US federal tax brackets, $16,100 Single / $32,200 Married Filing Jointly standard deductions, and a $184,500 Social Security wage base cap. Quarterly Tax Estimator also models 2025 safe harbor thresholds across the four IRS due dates.

Does Invoice Generator save client data or uploaded logos to localStorage?

No. Unlike the other eleven calculators, Invoice Generator stores nothing in localStorage and offers no shareable URL. Uploaded 16:9 letterhead images and invoice fields remain in memory until printed or closed.

Do these calculators replace a CPA, lender, or financial advisor?

No. Every calculator provides educational and planning estimates from your inputs without financial, tax, or legal advice. Verify final figures against official statements, lenders, or a qualified tax professional.

Can I install the calculators for offline use?

Yes. tools.builtbyjoshstudio.com registers a service worker with network-first caching and offline fallback for shared assets and visited pages, and works offline directly in modern browsers.
