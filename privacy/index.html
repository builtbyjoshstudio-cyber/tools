Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · Built by Josh Studio LLC, d/b/a Tynkr Tools & Co

The short version: the apps send us nothing, these websites count visits with Google Analytics, purchases happen on the store you buy from, and your email only exists on Substack if you chose to give it to them. Here is the whole story.

Who we are

This policy covers builtbyjoshstudio.com, tools.builtbyjoshstudio.com, tynkr.builtbyjoshstudio.com, and the Tynkr desktop apps (Ledger, Vault, Hearth, and Forge). The same text appears on all three sites.

Built by Josh Studio LLC (Kansas Business ID 10076138), d/b/a Tynkr Tools & Co, runs these sites and apps and is responsible for this policy. Questions or requests: support@builtbyjoshstudio.com.

The Tynkr apps send us nothing

Tynkr Ledger, Vault, Hearth, and Forge are local-first desktop apps. There are no accounts, no telemetry, no analytics, and no cloud. Everything you enter is stored in files on your own computer, and we have no way to see, receive, or recover any of it.

Available only through the Microsoft Store. The Tynkr apps are sold and delivered only through the Microsoft Store. The apps perform no network activity of their own: no update check, no license check, no analytics. Microsoft handles the purchase, the license, and updates, under Microsoft's privacy statement.

Your data folder is yours. You can open, export, change, or delete your data folder at any time. Uninstalling an app leaves your data folder in place, so nothing is lost by accident; delete the folder yourself if you want it gone.

Password protection. If you turn on password protection, your data is encrypted on your device. We never receive your password or your recovery key.

If you place an app's data folder or backups inside a synced folder (OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive), that copy is governed by that provider's terms — the choice, and the folder, are yours.

What these websites collect

Analytics. All three sites use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate traffic — pages visited, approximate region, device type, and which buttons get used (for example, buy and play buttons). Google sets cookies to do this and processes the data under Google's privacy policy. We see aggregate reports, never a profile of you. The sites work fine with an ad blocker or with cookies disabled — we won't nag you about it.

Hosting. The sites are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard web-server request data (like IP addresses) per the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Fonts. Our fonts are served from our own sites, not from Google Fonts or any other font service.

Saved in your browser. Some tools on tools.builtbyjoshstudio.com save what you enter (like your last recipe) in your browser's local storage so it's there next time, and the tools and Tynkr sites remember your light or dark choice the same way. That stays on your device and is never sent to us. The tools site can also keep a copy of its pages in your browser so the tools work offline. Clearing your browser's data for the site removes all of it.

That's it. The sites have no accounts, no comment system, and no forms that submit to us.

The email list lives on Substack

If you join the newsletter, the signup happens on Substack — our signup forms just send you there, sometimes with the email you typed filled in. Your email address is stored and processed by Substack under Substack's privacy policy; we see it only inside our Substack dashboard and use it for nothing except sending the newsletter. Every email has one-click unsubscribe.

Buying from us

We sell through:

  • Lemon Squeezy — templates, workbooks, the cookbook, and zodiac art on builtbyjoshstudio.com. Product pages load Lemon Squeezy's checkout script from Lemon Squeezy's servers when the page opens; zodiac collection pages load it only when you click Buy. You check out in Lemon Squeezy's window.
  • Etsy — some of the same products, through links to our Etsy listings.
  • Microsoft Store — the Tynkr desktop apps.

These stores collect your name, email address, and payment details under their own privacy policies. Etsy and Lemon Squeezy share your name, email address, and order details with us so we can deliver your order and answer your questions. The Microsoft Store does not share your name or email with us; we see only sales and download totals. We never see or store your payment card details.

Links to our Redbubble shop and to our books on Amazon take you to those sites; anything you buy there is handled under their own privacy policies.

YouTube videos (builtbyjoshstudio.com)

Videos on builtbyjoshstudio.com — book trailers, character videos, kitchen videos, and product walkthroughs — are click-to-load. The page shows a picture stored on our own site and makes no request to YouTube or Google until you press play. Then it loads YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com), and from that point YouTube and Google may set cookies and process data under Google's privacy policy.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent, or share personal information for advertising.
  • We never run third-party ads or ad trackers.
  • We never receive the data you put into a Tynkr app — by design, we can't.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep order information only as long as we need it to deliver your purchase and meet tax and legal obligations. If you email us, we keep that conversation only as long as needed to help you.

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete any personal information we hold about you by emailing support@builtbyjoshstudio.com. Information held by Etsy, Lemon Squeezy, Microsoft, Substack, or Google is on their systems; you can use their own tools, and we'll help where we can.

Children

These sites and apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address, and on all three sites, with a new effective date. Because we collect so little, changes should be rare and boring.
