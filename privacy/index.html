The Tynkr apps send us nothing

Tynkr Ledger, Vault, Hearth, and Forge are local-first desktop apps. There are no accounts, no telemetry, no analytics, and no cloud. Everything you enter is stored in files on your own computer, and we have no way to see, receive, or recover any of it.

Available only through the Microsoft Store. The Tynkr apps are sold and delivered only through the Microsoft Store. Tynkr Ledger, Vault, Hearth, and Forge perform no network activity of their own: no update check, no license check, no analytics. Tynkr Inkpress makes one kind of connection, described in its section below. Microsoft handles the purchase, the license, and updates, under Microsoft's privacy statement.

Microsoft may provide us with aggregate sales and review data, and crash reports if an app crashes, through its Partner Center developer tools, under Microsoft's privacy statement. If an app crashes, Windows may also send a crash report to Microsoft, depending on your Windows diagnostic settings.

Your data folder is yours. You can open, export, change, or delete your data folder at any time. Uninstalling an app leaves your data folder in place, so nothing is lost by accident; delete the folder yourself if you want it gone.

Password protection. In Tynkr Ledger, Vault, Hearth, and Forge, if you turn on password protection, your data is encrypted on your device. We never receive your password or your recovery key.

If you place an app's data folder or backups inside a synced folder (OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive), that copy is governed by that provider's terms — the choice, and the folder, are yours.

Tynkr Inkpress

Tynkr Inkpress works differently from the other four apps, so it has its own section.

It sends us nothing. Inkpress has no account, no telemetry, and no analytics. Your manuscripts, book settings, and exports stay on your PC.

Where your files are. Preferences, the recent-books list, the sample book, and default exports are in Documents\Tynkr Inkpress. Each book's settings are in a .inkpress.json file next to its manuscript. These are ordinary files protected by your Windows account; Inkpress does not encrypt them. Uninstalling Inkpress doesn't remove them.

Internet. The app's own code makes no connections, with one exception. When you make a print PDF, any image your manuscript links to on the web is downloaded from the site that hosts it, so that site can see your IP address. Use images stored on your PC to avoid this.

Microsoft components. The window is drawn by the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, which sends diagnostic data to Microsoft under your Windows diagnostic-data settings. PDF export runs Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome in the background, under their own privacy terms.

Microsoft Defender SmartScreen. Tynkr Inkpress includes Microsoft Defender SmartScreen, which collects and sends your information to Microsoft as disclosed in the Microsoft Privacy Statement (https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement) and in the SmartScreen section of User data and privacy in Microsoft Edge (https://learn.microsoft.com/legal/microsoft-edge/privacy#smartscreen).

What these websites collect

Analytics. Every site in the list above uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate traffic — pages visited, approximate region, device type, and which buttons get used (for example, buy and play buttons). Its script loads from Google’s servers as each page opens. Google sets cookies to do this and processes the data under Google’s privacy policy. We see aggregate reports, never a profile of you. The sites work fine with an ad blocker or with cookies disabled — we won’t nag you about it.

Videos. Videos — book trailers, character videos, cooking videos, and product walkthroughs — are click-to-load. Until you press play, the page shows a picture stored on our own site and makes no request to YouTube. When you press play, it loads YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com), and from that point YouTube and Google may set cookies and process data under Google’s privacy policy. (Analytics, above, is separate: it loads on every page.)

Checkout. Our checkout provider is Lemon Squeezy. Product pages load the checkout provider’s script from its servers when the page opens; some pages load it only when you click Buy. You check out in the checkout provider’s window, or on its checkout page if the window can’t open. What it collects, and what it shares with us, is under “Buying from us” below.

Saved in your browser. Some sites save things in your browser’s storage so they’re there next time: your color theme choice, what you enter into some tools (like your last recipe, an ingredient list, or a running brine timer), and, on sites that work offline, a copy of their pages, styles, and fonts so they open without a connection. That stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser’s data for a site removes all of it.

Newsletter signup. Signing up for the newsletter happens on Substack, not on our sites; see “The email list lives on Substack” below.

Hosting. The sites are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard web-server request data (like IP addresses) per the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Fonts. Our fonts are served from our own sites, not from Google Fonts or any other font service.

That's it. The sites have no accounts, no comment system, and no forms that submit to us.

The email list lives on Substack

If you join the newsletter, the signup happens on Substack — our signup forms just send you there, sometimes with the email you typed filled in. Your email address is stored and processed by Substack under Substack's privacy policy; we see it only inside our Substack dashboard and use it for nothing except sending the newsletter. Every email has one-click unsubscribe.

Buying from us

We sell through:

  • Our checkout provider — templates, workbooks, the cookbook, and zodiac art.
  • Etsy — some of the same products, through links to our Etsy listings.
  • Microsoft Store — the Tynkr desktop apps.

These stores collect your name, email address, and payment details under their own privacy policies. Etsy and our checkout provider share your name, email address, and order details with us so we can deliver your order and answer your questions. The Microsoft Store does not share your name or email with us; from the Microsoft Store we see only aggregate sales and download data, ratings and reviews, and crash reports, as described above. We never see or store your payment card details.

Links to our Redbubble shop and to our books on Amazon take you to those sites; anything you buy there is handled under their own privacy policies.

What we never do

  • We never run third-party ads or ad trackers.
  • We never receive the data you put into a Tynkr app — by design, we can't.
