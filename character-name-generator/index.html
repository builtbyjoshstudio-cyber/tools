Naming characters at the speed of play

Every game master knows the moment: the party ignores the plot, walks up to a background nobody, and asks their name. The pause that follows is where "Bob the Second Guard" is born. This generator exists for that pause — six names per click, fast enough to use mid-sentence, original enough that none of them arrive with someone else's lore attached.

Styles are vibes, not cultures

The four styles are sound-shapes, not species or nationalities: flowing names run long on vowels, guttural names bite down on hard consonants, short names fit farmhands and terriers, and noble names trail titles behind them. Mix freely — nothing here maps to any published setting's naming rules, which means nothing here contradicts them either.

Keep list, because the good ones vanish

The name you half-liked three clicks ago is gone forever unless you keep it. Hit Keep and it lands in a list that survives in your browser between sessions — a private stock of pre-approved names for the next time the party interrogates a fishmonger.

Are these names from any published game or setting?

No. Every name is assembled on the spot from invented syllable pools written for this tool — nothing is drawn from any publisher's setting, novel, or name list. If a generated name resembles an existing character, it's coincidence.

Can I use the generated names in my campaign or published work?

Yes. The names are procedurally generated original combinations — use them freely at your table or in your own writing.

Is this name generator free?

Yes — free, no ads, no signup, and it runs entirely in your browser. Your kept names are stored only on your own device.
